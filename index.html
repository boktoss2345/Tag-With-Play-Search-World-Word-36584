How To Play

1

Find all the hidden words listed for the level inside the letter grid.

2

Words can go left, right, up, down and diagonally — forwards or backwards.

3

Touch / Mouse: press the first letter and drag to the last letter, then release.

4

TV Remote / Keyboard: use the arrow keys to move the blue cursor, press OK / Enter to start selecting, move to the last letter, press OK / Enter again.

5

Found words are marked in the list and stay highlighted in a unique colour.

6

Use HINT to flash the first letter of a hidden word. You have 3 hints per level.

7

Press BACK / Escape to return or pause the game.

Scoring

Each word found gives 100 points. Finish fast for a time bonus, and use fewer hints to earn up to 3 stars per level.
